# Synthetic renal laboratory context checklist

Project-authored synthetic demonstration guidance, version 1. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. This document is a synthetic case review aid, not a dosing reference or validated clinical protocol.

## Creatinine and medication review context

A creatinine result flagged high may be relevant to clinician review of an active antibiotic prescription. Preserve the value, unit, collection date, and laboratory flag. A single creatinine value does not establish a new diagnosis or a trend. Baseline renal measurements, timing, and other relevant context may be missing. Identify these gaps explicitly; do not calculate an unsupported renal estimate or recommend a medication dose adjustment.

## Human review and evidence boundaries

Keep laboratory observations separate from guideline context. A retrieved passage is not evidence that a patient has a symptom or diagnosis. Cite the source document and section when using its checklist context. The final summary is clinician decision support for human review only. No autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, medication change, or downstream action follows from retrieval.

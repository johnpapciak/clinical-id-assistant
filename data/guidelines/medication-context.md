# Synthetic medication and allergy context checklist

Project-authored synthetic demonstration guidance, version 1. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. This document provides review context only and contains no prescribing recommendations.

## Active prescriptions and microbiology

For a synthetic microbiology review, active antibiotic prescriptions may be relevant context. Report the recorded drug, dose, route, frequency, and status exactly as supplied by the medication tool. A prescription record does not establish administration, adherence, indication, or clinical response. A susceptibility result describes an isolate and does not independently establish that a prescription is appropriate or inappropriate. Leave treatment decisions to the clinician.

## Recorded antibiotic allergies

When reviewing medication allergy context, preserve the recorded substance, reaction, and severity. Missing reaction details are unknown. An empty allergy list means no entries were recorded in this dataset, not confirmed absence of allergy. Ask the clinician to clarify relevant unknown history. Do not infer cross-reactivity, substitute an antibiotic, or recommend starting, stopping, or changing a medication.

# Synthetic urine culture review checklist

Project-authored synthetic demonstration guidance, version 1. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. This is a review checklist for synthetic cases, not a validated clinical guideline or treatment protocol.

## Urine culture interpretation context

An Escherichia coli (E. coli) urine culture records a laboratory observation. Culture growth alone does not establish symptomatic infection or a need for antibiotics. A reviewer needs symptoms, specimen collection context, and the reason testing was obtained. If these details were not returned by patient tools, list them as missing information. Preserve the specimen, collection date, organism, quantity, and reported qualifiers without inventing additional context.

## Previous microbiology and infection history

Previous urine cultures can provide historical organism and susceptibility context. A previous positive culture is distinct from an explicitly documented infection. Keep dates and specimen sites visible, and distinguish recorded infection diagnoses from culture observations. Do not assume an older susceptibility applies to the current isolate. Any comparison is information for a clinician to review, not an instruction to select or change therapy.
